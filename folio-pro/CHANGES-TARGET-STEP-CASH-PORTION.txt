Target Planner — Partial Cash Allocation Per Investment Step

- Added an Investment Portion (%) field to every reinvestment step.
- New steps default to 100% to preserve the previous behavior.
- Existing saved steps without an allocation value also behave as 100%.
- The return is applied only to the allocated portion of cash.
- Uninvested cash is carried forward into the next step.
- Each step now displays Amount Invested, Cash Carried Forward, Estimated Value After Step, and step profit.
- Allocation is constrained between 0% and 100%.
