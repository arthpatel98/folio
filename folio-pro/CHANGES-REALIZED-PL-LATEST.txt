Realized P/L updates
- Deleting a stock/option entry now persists even when the entry originated from transaction history.
- If the removed entry is the final stock/option entry for a ticker, the ticker disappears automatically.
- Tickers with exactly $0.00 realized P/L are included in Profitable Tickers.
- Added ticker-level Edit action beside the ticker summary row.
- Ticker-level comments are now stored independently at ticker level.
