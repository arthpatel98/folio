Folio Pro — Option Cash, Position Simulator, Savings, and Login Update

1. Short option removal cash rules
- Sell Call removal: stored cash decreases by absolute contracts × current/close price.
- Sell Put removal: stored cash decreases by absolute contracts × current/close price.
- Sell Put collateral remains derived from open positions. Removing/closing contracts therefore releases strike × contracts × 100 into displayed available cash automatically, without double-adding collateral to stored cash.
- Applied to both direct Holdings removal and transaction-style option close paths.

2. Position Simulator
- Potential Sell Price resets to the latest current Holdings price whenever a position/ticker is selected or reselected.
- A manual scenario price remains usable while staying on that position, but does not persist after switching away and back.

3. Target Planner
- Renamed button to Add Investment Step.
- Added no-wrap/shrink safeguards so button text stays aligned.

4. Savings & Investments
- Added light dashed gridlines to YTD Performance.
- Yearly Realized Income now follows the selected portfolio (Robinhood, Fidelity Roth IRA, Fidelity 401(k), or All Portfolios).
- Added yearly breakdowns for Profit and Dividend, Interest & Bonus.
- 2026 Realized YTD and Realized Income summary values now follow the selected portfolio for consistency.
- Removed Workbook yearly totals.
- Removed Realized profit + DIV / INT / extras.
- Removed Savings & Investments workbook.
- Removed Investment performance using your workbook investment basis and live portfolio values from Holdings.
- Updated visible Savings page wording to Title Case, including Profit Vs Dividend, Interest & Bonus By Quarter.

5. Authentication and homepage routing
- Signed-out visits to the app redirect to /login.
- Signed-in visits to /login redirect to /holdings.
- Root / redirects authenticated users to /holdings.
- Google and email sign-in callbacks now land on /holdings.
- Supabase session cookies continue to persist, so users remain signed in until they explicitly sign out or the session becomes invalid.
