Realized P/L updates
- Search now matches both ticker symbols and ticker-level comments.
- Deleting a stock/option realized entry now also suppresses matching transaction/fee-backed rows so deleted tickers do not reappear.
- If no stock/option entries remain for a ticker, the ticker disappears from Realized P/L By Ticker.
- Summary cards are arranged as two rows of three:
  1. Total Realized P/L
  2. Total Stocks P/L
  3. Total Options P/L
  4. Total Dividend Amount
  5. Profitable Tickers
  6. Loss Recovery Tickers
- Total Dividend Amount is the sum of all dividend amounts in the active portfolio view.
